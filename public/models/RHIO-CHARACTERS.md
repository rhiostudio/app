# RHIO character collection (v2, 28 Sep 2026)

25 original procedural characters, rendered live with three.js by the engine in
lib/rhio3d (sources in lib/rhio3d/src). No model files are downloaded.

Humanoids (22): Atlas, Nova, Orbit, Kira, Dash, Byte, Echo, Terra, Volt, Vesper, Rook,
Mira, Juno, Cole, Lumi, Otto, Wren, Zara, Felix, Ines, Taro, Noor.
Companions (3): Scout, Maker, Guardian.

Outfit system: build (slim/regular/curvy/broad), skin, eyes, 13 hair styles, facial hair,
12 tops, 5 bottoms, legwear, 3 shoe types, head/face/back gear, colors, glow and finish.
Motions (20, all characters): Idle, Wave, Nod, Shrug, Think, Point, Clap, Cheer, Laugh, Bow,
Salute, Stretch, Typing, Walk, Run, Jump, Spin, Dance, Disco, Victory.
Powers (6): Pulse Orb, Aegis, Blink, Levitate, Deep Scan, Hype. Each character has a signature power.

Motions are original procedural animation, not motion capture. Walk/Run circle the pad.
The GLB files in this folder are the first-generation characters, kept for provenance only.
